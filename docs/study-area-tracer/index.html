Georeference this outline

Match known locations on the PDF to their coordinates. This lets the tracer place the orange outline on a map.

  1. Place a point. Click Mark control on PDF, then click the exact crossing of two labelled grid lines. You can also use an identifiable location with coordinates from a reference map. A scale bar alone cannot locate the panel. If someone already saved the calibration, use Load calibration JSON or GeoJSON and start at step 4.
  2. Fill in the new row. Enter longitude first and latitude second, in WGS 84 decimal degrees. For example, 19° W and 21°30′ N are −19 and 21.5. West and south are negative. In Source / note, you can record the grid label or reference used.
  3. Choose Fit or Check. Repeat until you have at least three Fit points spread around the outline, with at least one off the line joining the others. These locate the outline. Add at least one different known location and select Check in its row. The tracer leaves it out of the fit and reports how far its predicted location is from the known location. Without a separate Check point, the downloads stay locked.
  4. Inspect and correct. Compare the markers and coordinates with the PDF or your reference. Use Move to click a marker's correct location, or edit its coordinates in the row. Read Error (km) for the Check rows. In the preview, look for orange sections beyond the dotted grey boundary, where the position is estimated beyond the Fit points.
  5. Complete the checklist below. Tick each item only after checking it. If you cannot verify the location or the errors are too large for the intended map, leave the review incomplete. Both downloads unlock when the review is complete.
